# Before he spoke
**Design is translation.**  
From silence to meaning. From emotion to expression. From difference to connection.

---

## Hello!

Hi, my name is **Chava**.  
I’m a graphic designer, marketer, illustrator, entertainer — and most importantly, a **storyteller**.  
And I learned early that the most important messages aren’t always spoken.

---

## This is my brother!

Well… this is what he looked like as a kid.  
He’s now an adult man — tall, loud, VERY opinionated and, admitidly by my own fault, sarcastic.

But why is he important?

Because he taught me everything I know about **empathy**, **creativity**, and the power of **design and communication**.

---

## The diagnoses

My brother was diagnosed with:

- **Autism** — a neurological condition that affects how people interact, communicate, and behave  
- **Dysphasia** — a language disorder that makes speaking, understanding, reading, and writing difficult

This is why, for most of his childhood, he was **non-verbal**.

---

## Silent conversations

One would think, that it was hard to communicate with my brother, and at first it was.
But he wasn’t silent — he just couldn’t use words.

But everything he did had meaning:
- A glance  
- A repeated sound  
- A drawing  
- A meltdown  

These were his **fonts** and **formats**.

---

## Designing speech

We used communication cards — but they were limited.  
So I started drawing new ones, just for him.

I became his **translator**.  
Long before I knew what design was.

And without realizing it, I was learning the foundations of **UX**.

---

## Connecting the pieces

When I discovered design, everything made sense.

Design isn’t just about making things look nice —  
It’s about **communicating clearly** when the default language doesn’t work.

Just like with my brother, I had to think about:
- How others perceive  
- How they process  
- How they interact

**Empathy** isn’t an extra step — it’s the whole darn job.

---

## *CLICK*

Experts working with non-verbal kids agree:
- Meet people where they are  
- Be patient  
- Get creative  

Visuals. Routines. Consistency.  
It’s all **UX**.

My brother’s childhood?  
My first **usability test**.  
And he was the most honest user I’ve ever had.

---

## Speaking of...
Today, I design not just for **clarity**, but for **inclusion**.

- Visual systems  
- Tools for communities  
- Characters who speak without words

Because of him:
- I ask better questions  
- I design better systems  
- I **listen**

---

## Listening, not just hearing

Growing up with my brother showed me:  
Everyone is communicating — just not always in ways we expect.

As designers, our job is to listen:  
Not just with our ears.  
But with our **eyes**, our **minds**, and our **hearts**.

---

## Now he speaks

My brother speaks now. A lot.  
As I said before, he’s funny, sarcastic, and full of opinions.

Back then, to ask for ice cream, he showed us a picture.  
Now he just goes:  
> “Wanna hang out and get some ice cream?”

A lot changed.  
But those wordless conversations became the **loudest influence** on who I am as a designer.

---

## The Big Idea

**Design is translation.**  
Not just from idea to image —  
But from emotion to expression, silence to meaning, and difference to connection.

Growing up with my non-verbal brother taught me how to see, how to listen, and how to design.  
**Long before I ever opened Adobe.**
